# MadhvaNidhi Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 August 2026

MadhvaNidhi is developed by Human Centric Works, Hospet, India. The app is an
offline-first Panchanga and Madhva devotional reader.

## Data handled by the app

MadhvaNidhi does not require an account and does not collect names, email
addresses, contacts, precise location, advertising identifiers, payment data, or
analytics. Veda choice, Panchanga tradition, reading state, favourites, Japa
counts, alarms, and other preferences are stored locally on the user's device.

When the Sri Raghavendra Swamy Matha Panchanga tradition is selected and an
internet connection is available, the app requests the selected calendar date
from the official `srsmatha.org` Panchanga page. That website may receive normal
network information such as IP address, request time, and user agent. MadhvaNidhi
does not add personal identifiers to the request. Other traditions use an
on-device calculation in version 1.0.

## Sharing and sale

Human Centric Works does not sell or share user data. The app contains no ads and
no third-party analytics SDK.

## Permissions

Internet access is used for official Panchanga retrieval. Notifications,
vibration, wake lock, and boot completion are used only for user-created alarms
and reminders. Users can deny notification permission or remove alarms at any
time.

## Retention and deletion

App preferences remain on the device until the user clears app storage or
uninstalls MadhvaNidhi. Human Centric Works does not maintain a server-side user
profile to delete.

## Children

MadhvaNidhi is a general-audience religious reference app and is not directed
specifically to children. It does not knowingly collect personal information
from children.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact Human Centric Works through <https://hcworks.in>.

This policy may be updated when app functionality or legal requirements change.
The current version will be published on the Human Centric Works website.
