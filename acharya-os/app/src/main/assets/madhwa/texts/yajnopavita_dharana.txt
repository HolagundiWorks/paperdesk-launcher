2. What the Practice Is

Yajñopavīta Dhāraṇa is the ritual wearing of the sacred thread.

In the Madhwa-specific source used here, the procedure contains:

1. Ācamana
2. Prāṇāyāma
3. Saṅkalpa
4. Yajñopavīta saṃskāra / preparation
5. Yajñopavīta prāṇa-pratiṣṭhāpana
6. Yajñopavīta mantra viniyoga
7. Yajñopavīta dhāraṇa
8. Jīrṇa yajñopavīta visarjana
9. Ācamana after visarjana
10. Madhwa-sampradaya concluding dedication

The source specifically says that if more than one yajñopavīta is worn, the same dhāraṇa mantra is recited for each one. It gives separate saṅkalpa wording for the second, third and fourth yajñopavīta. 

3. Preliminary — Śarīra Śuddhi

Sanskrit

अपवित्रः पवित्रो वा सर्वावस्थां गतोऽपि वा ।
यः स्मरेत् पुण्डरीकाक्षं स बाह्याभ्यन्तरः शुचिः ॥

Kannada

ಅಪವಿತ್ರಃ ಪವಿತ್ರೋ ವಾ ಸರ್ವಾವಸ್ಥಾಂ ಗತೋಽಪಿ ವಾ ।
ಯಃ ಸ್ಮರೇತ್ ಪುಂಡರೀಕಾಕ್ಷಂ ಸ ಬಾಹ್ಯಾಭ್ಯಂತರಃ ಶುಚಿಃ ॥

English

Whether impure or pure, or in whatever condition one may be, one who remembers Puṇḍarīkākṣa becomes purified both externally and internally.

5. Prāṇāyāma — ಪ್ರಾಣಾಯಾಮ

The Madhwa-specific source places prāṇāyāma before saṅkalpa. 

6. Saṅkalpa — ಸಂಕಲ್ಪ

The source gives a long traditional saṅkalpa containing the cosmic time/location descriptors and a Madhwa-specific dedication.

Core purpose

Sanskrit / Transliteration

श्री भारतीरमण मुख्यप्राणान्तर्गत
श्री वेदव्यास प्रेरणया
श्री वेदव्यास प्रीत्यर्थम्
श्रौत स्मार्त कर्मानुष्ठान योग्यता सिद्ध्यर्थम्
यज्ञोपवीतधारणमहं करिष्ये ।

तदादौ यज्ञोपवीत संस्कारं करिष्ये ॥

Kannada

ಶ್ರೀ ಭಾರತೀರಮಣ ಮುಖ್ಯಪ್ರಾಣಾಂತರ್ಗತ
ಶ್ರೀ ವೇದವ್ಯಾಸ ಪ್ರೇರಣಯಾ
ಶ್ರೀ ವೇದವ್ಯಾಸ ಪ್ರೀತ್ಯರ್ಥಮ್
ಶ್ರೌತ ಸ್ಮಾರ್ತ ಕರ್ಮಾನುಷ್ಠಾನ ಯೋಗ್ಯತಾ ಸಿದ್ಧ್ಯರ್ಥಮ್
ಯಜ್ಞೋಪವೀತಧಾರಣಮಹಂ ಕರಿಷ್ಯೇ ।

ತದಾದೌ ಯಜ್ಞೋಪವೀತ ಸಂಸ್ಕಾರಂ ಕರಿಷ್ಯೇ ॥

English

For the inspiration of Sri Vedavyasa, who is within Sri Bharati-Ramana and Mukhya-Prana, and for the pleasure of Sri Vedavyasa, and for attaining eligibility to perform Śrauta and Smārta rites, I shall perform the wearing of the Yajñopavīta.

First, I shall perform the consecration/preparation of the Yajñopavīta.

This purpose statement follows the Madhwa-specific source. 

7. Yajñopavīta Saṃskāra

The source places Yajñopavīta saṃskāra immediately before the actual wearing.

The exact family procedure for preparing, touching, arranging or sanctifying the thread may vary. The app should therefore present this as a separate step rather than silently supplying a universal physical procedure.

8. Yajñopavīta Prāṇa-Pratiṣṭhāpana

Saṅkalpa

Sanskrit

यज्ञोपवीत प्राण प्रतिष्ठापनं करिष्ये ।

Kannada

ಯಜ್ಞೋಪವೀತ ಪ್ರಾಣ ಪ್ರತಿಷ್ಠಾಪನಂ ಕರಿಷ್ಯೇ ।

English

I shall perform the prāṇa-pratiṣṭhāpana of the Yajñopavīta.

Ṛgvedic mantra

Sanskrit

ॐ असुनीते पुनरस्मासु चक्षुः
पुनः प्राणमिह नो धेहि भोगम् ।
ज्योक् पश्येम सूर्यमुच्चरन्तम्
अनुमते मृडया नः स्वस्ति ॥

ऋग्वेद 10.59.6

Kannada

ಓಂ ಅಸು ನೀತೇ ಪುನರಸ್ಮಾಸು ಚಕ್ಷುಃ
ಪುನಃ ಪ್ರಾಣಮಿಹ ನೋ ಧೇಹಿ ಭೋಗಮ್ ।
ಜ್ಯೋಕ್ ಪಶ್ಯೇಮ ಸೂರ್ಯಮುಚ್ಚರಂತಮ್
ಅನುಮತೇ ಮೃಡಯಾ ನಃ ಸ್ವಸ್ತಿ ॥

English / Transliteration

Om asunīte punar asmāsu cakṣuḥ

jyok paśyema sūryam uccarantam  
anumate mṛḍayā naḥ svasti 

The source identifies this as Ṛgveda 10.59.6. 

Follow-up statement

Sanskrit

अमृतं वै प्राणाः ।
अमृतमापः ।
प्राणानेव यथास्थानमुपह्वयते ॥

English

The source follows the mantra with a statement concerning the immortal nature of prāṇa and waters and the invocation of the prāṇas to their proper place. 

9. Yajñopavīta Mantra — Viniyoga

Sanskrit

यज्ञोपवीते तस्य मन्त्रस्य
परमेष्ठी परब्रह्म ऋषिः ।
परमात्मा देवता ।
देवी गायत्री छन्दः ।
यज्ञोपवीत धारणे विनियोगः ॥

Kannada

ಯಜ್ಞೋಪವೀತೇ ತಸ್ಯ ಮಂತ್ರಸ್ಯ
ಪರಮೇಷ್ಠೀ ಪರಬ್ರಹ್ಮ ಋಷಿಃ ।
ಪರಮಾತ್ಮಾ ದೇವತಾ ।
ದೇವೀ ಗಾಯತ್ರೀ ಛಂದಃ ।
ಯಜ್ಞೋಪವೀತ ಧಾರಣೇ ವಿನಿಯೋಗಃ ॥

English

For the Yajñopavīta mantra:

- Ṛṣi: Parameṣṭhī / Parabrahma
- Devatā: Paramātma
- Chandas: Gāyatrī
- Application: Yajñopavīta Dhāraṇa

This viniyoga is given in the published Yajñopavīta Dhāraṇa text. 

10. Main Yajñopavīta Dhāraṇa Mantra

Sanskrit

ॐ

यज्ञोपवीतं परमं पवित्रं
प्रजापतेर्यत्सहजं पुरस्तात् ।
आयुष्यमग्र्यं प्रतिमुञ्च शुभ्रं
यज्ञोपवीतं बलमस्तु तेजः ॥

Kannada

ಓಂ

ಯಜ್ಞೋಪವೀತಂ ಪರಮಂ ಪವಿತ್ರಂ
ಪ್ರಜಾಪತೇರ್ಯತ್ಸಹಜಂ ಪುರಸ್ತಾತ್ ।
ಆಯುಷ್ಯಮಗ್ರ್ಯಂ ಪ್ರತಿಮುಂಚ ಶುಭ್ರಂ
ಯಜ್ಞೋಪವೀತಂ ಬಲಮಸ್ತು ತೇಜಃ ॥

Transliteration

Om

yajñopavītaṃ paramaṃ pavitraṃ  
prajāpateryat-sahajaṃ purastāt |  
āyuṣyam-agryaṃ pratimuñca śubhraṃ  
yajñopavītaṃ balam-astu tejaḥ 

English Meaning

The Yajñopavīta is supremely pure, born together with Prajāpati and present from the beginning. May this pure sacred thread bestow foremost longevity, strength and spiritual radiance.

The published Madhwa source instructs that this mantra is recited while wearing the janivara; it also says that the same mantra is recited for each additional Yajñopavīta. 

11. Wearing More Than One Yajñopavīta

The Madhwa-specific source provides distinct saṅkalpa statements.

Second Yajñopavīta

Sanskrit / Transliteration

गृहस्थाश्रमसिद्ध्यर्थे
द्वितीय यज्ञोपवीतधारणं करिष्ये ॥

English:  
For the accomplishment/requirements of Gṛhastha Āśrama, I shall wear the second Yajñopavīta.

Third Yajñopavīta

Sanskrit / Transliteration

उत्तरीयार्थे
तृतीय यज्ञोपवीत धारणं करिष्ये ॥

English:  
For the purpose of the uttarīya, I shall wear the third Yajñopavīta.

Fourth Yajñopavīta

Sanskrit / Transliteration

सहायार्थं
चतुर्थ यज्ञोपवीत धारणं करिष्ये ॥

English:  
For assistance/support, I shall wear the fourth Yajñopavīta.

These three purposes are explicitly given in the Sumadhwa Seva source. 

12. Jīrṇa Yajñopavīta Visarjana — Old Thread Removal

The source instructs that the old yajñopavīta is removed separately.

It also states that at least ten Gāyatrī mantras are to be chanted before the old thread is discarded. 

Sanskrit

उपवीतं भिन्नतन्तुं जीर्णं कश्मलदूषितम् ।
विसृजामि परब्रह्मन्
वर्चो दीर्घायुरस्तु मे ॥

Kannada

ಉಪವೀತಂ ಭಿನ್ನತಂತುಂ ಜೀರ್ಣಂ ಕಶ್ಮಲದೂಷಿತಮ್ ।
ವಿಸೃಜಾಮಿ ಪರಬ್ರಹ್ಮನ್
ವರ್ಚೋ ದೀರ್ಘಾಯುರಸ್ತು ಮೇ ॥

Transliteration

upavītaṃ bhinnatantuṃ jīrṇaṃ kaśmaladūṣitam |  
visṛjāmi parabrahman  
varco dīrghāyur astu me 

English Meaning

This old, worn and impurity-affected sacred thread, whose thread has become broken, I relinquish. May spiritual radiance and long life be mine.

13. Disposal of the Old Yajñopavīta

Sanskrit / Transliteration

“समुद्रं गच्छ स्वाहा”

samudraṃ gaccha svāhā

The Madhwa-specific source gives this as the statement accompanying the disposal of the old Yajñopavīta. 

Kannada

“ಸಮುದ್ರಂ ಗಚ್ಛ ಸ್ವಾಹಾ”

English

“Go to the ocean, svāhā.”

14. Punara-Ācamana — ಪುನರಾಚಮನ

After disposal of the old thread, the source instructs another ācamana.

The abbreviated concluding names given are:

ॐ अच्युताय नमः ।
ॐ अनन्ताय नमः ।
ॐ गोविन्दाय नमः ॥

Kannada

ಓಂ ಅಚ್ಯುತಾಯ ನಮಃ ।
ಓಂ ಅನಂತಾಯ ನಮಃ ।
ಓಂ ಗೋವಿಂದಾಯ ನಮಃ ॥

Transliteration

Om Acyutāya namaḥ |  
Om Anantāya namaḥ |  
Om Govindāya namaḥ 

The three-name form is specifically recorded in the Madhwa Yajñopavīta source. 

15. Madhwa Sampradaya Closing Dedication

This is especially important for the app because the source is explicitly Madhwa-oriented.

Sanskrit / Transliteration

अनेन यज्ञोपवीतधारणेन
भगवान् मध्वान्तर्गत
श्री विष्णु प्रेरणया
श्री विष्णु प्रीत्यर्थं
प्रीतो वरदो भवतु ।

श्री कृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥

Kannada

ಅನೇನ ಯಜ್ಞೋಪವೀತಧಾರಣೇನ
ಭಗವಾನ್ ಮಧ್ವಾಂತರ್ಗತ
ಶ್ರೀ ವಿಷ್ಣು ಪ್ರೇರಣಯಾ
ಶ್ರೀ ವಿಷ್ಣು ಪ್ರೀತ್ಯರ್ಥಂ
ಪ್ರೀತೋ ವರದೋ ಭವತು ।

ಶ್ರೀ ಕೃಷ್ಣಾರ್ಪಣಮಸ್ತು ॥

English

By this Yajñopavīta Dhāraṇa, may Bhagavān, with Sri Viṣṇu within Sri Madhva as the indwelling controller, be pleased and grant His blessing for the pleasure of Sri Viṣṇu.

May this be offered to Sri Krishna.

The Madhwa-specific wording is documented in the Sumadhwa Seva source. 

16. Alternative / Expanded Samarpana

Some published Yajñopavīta Dhāraṇa versions include a general samarpana after the ritual.

Sanskrit

यस्य स्मृत्या च नामोक्त्या
तपःसन्ध्यादि क्रियादिषु ।
न्यूनं सम्पूर्णतां याति
सद्यो वन्दे तमच्युतम् ॥

मन्त्रहीनं क्रियाहीनं
भक्तिहीनं रमापते ।
यत्कृतं तु मया देव
परिपूर्णं तदस्तु मे ॥

Kannada

ಯಸ್ಯ ಸ್ಮೃತ್ಯಾ ಚ ನಾಮೋಕ್ತ್ಯಾ
ತಪಃಸಂಧ್ಯಾದಿ ಕ್ರಿಯದಿಷು ।
ನ್ಯೂನಂ ಸಂಪೂರ್ಣತಾಂ ಯಾತಿ
ಸದ್ಯೋ ವಂದೇ ತಮಚ್ಯುತಮ್ ॥

ಮಂತ್ರಹೀನಂ ಕ್ರಿಯಾಹೀನಂ
ಭಕ್ತಿಹೀನಂ ರಮಾಪತೇ ।
ಯತ್ಕೃತಂ ತು ಮಯಾ ದೇವ
ಪರಿಪೂರ್ಣಂ ತದಸ್ತು ಮೇ ॥

English

By whose remembrance and utterance of whose name deficiencies in austerity, Sandhyā and other rites become complete, I immediately bow to that Acyuta.

O consort of Ramā, whatever I have performed with deficiency in mantra, ritual or devotion, may that become complete.

This expanded samarpana appears in other published Yajñopavīta Dhāraṇa versions; it should therefore be marked in the app as an additional/variant closing, not silently substituted for the Madhwa-specific source wording.

Kannada

Kannada

ಕಾಯೇನ ವಾಚಾ ಮನಸೇಂದ್ರಿಯೈರ್ವಾ
ಬುದ್ಧ್ಯಾತ್ಮನಾ ವಾ ಪ್ರಕೃತೇಃ ಸ್ವಭಾವಾತ್ ।
ಕರೋಮಿ ಯದ್ಯತ್ ಸಕಲಂ ಪರಸ್ಮೈ
ಶ್ರೀಮನ್ನಾರಾಯಣಾಯೇತಿ ಸಮರ್ಪಯಾಮಿ ॥

English

Whatever I perform through body, speech, mind, senses, intellect, self or according to my natural disposition, I offer all of it to the Supreme Sri Mannarayana.

This is present in the expanded Yajñopavīta Dhāraṇa reference.

21. Source and Variant Policy

For the app, keep these separate:

Madhwa-specific source

Use the Sumadhwa Seva Yagnopaveeta Dharana Vidhi as the primary Madhwa version.

It specifically contains:

- Madhwa-oriented saṅkalpa
- Sri Vedavyasa dedication
- Additional Yajñopavīta purposes
- Old-thread removal
- Madhwa-specific concluding dedication
- Sri Krishna arpaṇa

General Yajñopavīta versions

Other sources contain:

- Expanded 24-name ācamana
- Expanded samarpana
- Lakshmi-Narayana dedication
- Kāyena Vācā final offering

These should be stored as variants, not merged into the Madhwa source without attribution.

23. Source References

1. Sumadhwa Seva — Yagnopaveeta Dharana Vidhi  
   Madhwa-specific source; attributed to Vidwan Sri S. N. Ramachandrachar and collected by Narahari Sumadhwa. 

2. Vaidika Vignanam — Yagnopavita Dharana  
   Expanded Sanskrit/IAST reference including ācamana, prāṇāyāma, prāṇa-pratiṣṭhāpana, dhāraṇa, visarjana and samarpana. 

3. Vedic Tithi — Yajnopavita Dharana  
   Independent reference for the dhāraṇa mantra, viniyoga and old-thread visarjana. 

24. App Content Integrity Note

Do not automatically alter Sanskrit spelling, split sandhi, change mantra wording, or combine variants.

For the final production database, every mantra should retain:

```yaml
source_text: true
source_url: ""
tradition: ""
variant: ""
language:
  kannada: ""
  devanagari: ""
  transliteration: ""
  english_meaning: ""
```

This allows the app to distinguish:

- Source Kannada
- Source Sanskrit
- Roman transliteration
- English meaning
- Madhwa-specific wording
- General/variant wording

and prevents different ritual traditions from accidentally being merged.
