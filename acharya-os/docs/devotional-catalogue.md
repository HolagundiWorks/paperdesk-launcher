# AcharyaOS devotional catalogue

## Tradition labels

- The dedicated Sandhyavandana page is **Rigvediya / Ashvalayana**, in Kannada, with separate morning, noon and evening alternatives inside the complete manual.
- Madhwa-specific texts retain their Madhwa attribution. General Sanskrit texts are not relabelled as Madhwa-only.
- Initiated, bija, nyasa and family-specific ritual instructions must follow the user's guru or family acharya.

## Coverage

- Rigveda suktas: Aghamarshana, Ayushya, Bhagya, Bhu, Purusha, Shri, Varuna and Vishnu.
- Madhwa essentials: Dvadasha Stotra, Hari Vayu Stuti, Nakha Stuti, Madhwa and yati texts.
- Daily parayana: Vishnu Sahasranama, Venkatesha Suprabhata, Aditya Hridaya and selected Narasimha stotras.
- Raghavendra stotras and mangala, Haridasa songs, Suladis and Ugabhogas.
- Deva Pooja, Yajnopavita, festival guidance and Rigvediya Trikala Sandhyavandana.

## Primary references retained

- `resources/reference/madhwa-devapooja-paddhati-kannada.pdf` — Madhwa Vaishnava Tantra Sarokta Deva Pooja Paddhati.
- `app/src/main/assets/madhwa/texts/rigveda_sandhyavandana.txt` — offline, cleaned source text with its source URL recorded in the header.

The catalogue audit must remain at zero missing files, orphan bodies, duplicate body groups and formatting-noise bodies.
